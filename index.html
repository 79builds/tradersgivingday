Why we chose Aerial Recovery

On Veterans Day, we give to the ones still serving.

Heal the Heroes

Aerial Recovery's Heal the Heroes program helps veterans and first responders find new purpose after service. Instead of leaving their training behind, they put it to work again.

  • Fighting human traffickingHeroes from the program join operations that rescue victims of trafficking.
  • Disaster rescue worldwideWhen natural disasters hit, they respond on the ground around the world.

Learn more at AerialRecovery.org or @aerialrecoverygroup on Instagram.
